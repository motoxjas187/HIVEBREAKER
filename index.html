HIVEBREAKER

You wake in a 4×4 pocket. Everything else is rock, and the dark hides an endless world of alien hives.

Dig by pushing into rock or aiming at it. Every block shows its health, and rock gets tougher the farther you go and the longer you survive. Ore buys rig upgrades. Gold relic blocks give one free.

Hives are buried in the rock. Kills drop guns and attachments that get better the deeper you go. Queens, brutes and hives drop Unique guns. Fuse and merge them in the ⚔ ARMS screen. A hive you destroy grows back once it's out of sight, and it sends raids that tunnel after you.
