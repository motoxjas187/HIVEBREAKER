HIVEBREAKER

You wake in a 4×4 pocket. Everything else is rock, and the dark hides an endless world of alien hives.

Dig by pushing into rock or aiming at it. Every block shows its health, and rock gets tougher the farther you go and the longer you survive. Ore fills your cargo hold: recall to base (⌂ BASE) to store it, smelt it into bars and forge rig upgrades. Gold relic blocks give one free.

Hives are buried in the rock. Kills drop guns and attachments that get better the deeper you go. Every hive is wrapped in a shell of tougher blocks that tells you its strength: purple chitin, amber resin, red flesh, then black carapace. Listen for it festering behind the rock. Queens, brutes and hives drop Unique guns. Fuse and merge them in the ⚔ ARMS screen. A hive you destroy grows back once it's out of sight, and it sends raids that tunnel after you.

Touch: left side moves, right side aims · Keys: WASD, mouse aims, click fires
